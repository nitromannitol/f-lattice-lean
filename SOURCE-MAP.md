## First proof's inputs

For `IsBurningRank.allowed`, the implicit field and rank, rank carrier and order are typing inputs from Section 9. The rank hypothesis expands to the two cases in Section 9 at every physical site. The finite vertex set and its nonemptiness are the induced subgraph in the conclusion of that argument.

For `finiteComparison_of_allowed`, the two fields and the finite set are the comparison data. The allowed-set hypothesis is exactly the forward-use hypothesis in `prop:recurrence`. The integer bound quantifies the pointwise form, with one hypothesis requiring that bound on the incoming boundary. The competitor assumption is restricted to the set because the proof only uses its Laplacian there. `IsBurningRank.finiteComparison` supplies the allowed-set hypothesis from the rank, and the separate endpoint module supplies the rank from the explicit endpoint function.

## Ordered height rule inputs

The ambient carrier in `HeightTile` is generic; the physical specialization is `Site`. Decidable equality is used only for finite support operations. The two tile heights have values on their supports, not elsewhere. The contact input to `contactShift` is needed for the minimum and follows from the equality witness of `Fits`. The shared target and old gauge represent $T+c$. A single offset in `Fits` is measured relative to $T$; `contactShift_eq_of_agrees` subtracts the old gauge from it, so the actual shift is measured relative to $T+c$. The new-site equality, overlap inequality and attained equality occur in that order in `Fits`. There is no hypothesis of agreement at every overlap site.

`OrderedComparisons` expands to these three tests at each insertion on the current prefix's union. It does not establish them for any source-specific list. `correctionHeight` acts on an already placed list (with nonempty first copy and the required contacts); it does not choose either correction placement or its short/long list.

The physical placement type has only finite occupied sites and integral height data. `place` uses the injective map $x\mapsto x+v$ on the entire finite support and retrieves each height at $x$ before adding the slope evaluated at $x+v$. The extrema used by trim and lower-left centering require nonempty support, as in the constructor. Trim takes separate coordinate minima, while lower-left and upper-right are extrema in row-then-column order. `meldPlaced` and `meldCorrection` consume the exact insertion rule with the same prefix contacts and then apply the respective source finishing operations; the agreement target is pulled back by the actual rectangular origin.

The initializers range over the empty word or a positive pure-run length `extra + 1`, not arbitrary words. The given lower and upper coordinates of a literal mask bound its enumerating rectangle; masking inside it retains exactly the writes and honors overwrite priority (including the alternate ladder diagonal rule that omits the fourth height term). The denominator guard is exactly odd $d\ge3$ for staircases and even $d\ge4$ for ladders. Nonemptiness is proved before trim, not assumed of the source-specific masks. The root is separate; neither pure family runs through a nonexistent empty correction ancestor.

## Farey chronology inputs

`parentsAt` folds the three exact parent selections from the root. The two current registers are respectively $O+E$ and $2O+E$ in integer coordinates. The unimodular invariant proves that rational normalization does not change the generated coordinates. There is only one inverse birth for each interior reduced fraction: `existsUnique_birth` takes positivity, the strict upper bound on the slope, and coprimality. `existsUnique_rational_birth` takes only the interior inequalities on the normalized rational. The inverse computes in the two positive parent coefficients, with strict decrease of their sum, not a depth budget. The endpoint atoms have no current birth.

`retainedBirth?` returns the unique earlier current birth on the same lineage, as required by `_recbirth`, or none for the endpoint. It is distinct from a correction source: `AncestorHistory.readCorrection` reads a word from the *pre-update* state initialized according to `splitInitial`. The rigid pure suffix invariants show that its saved word is the correct kind of prefix and is nonempty when the current word is nonempty. These proofs concern the word components only, not the geometry or tile data at the saved states.

## Alternate midpoint inputs

In `isBurningRank_roundedMidpoint`, the two fields, rank and comparison relation on ranks are the source's plane sheets and typing data. `hsecond` is the binary Laplacian on the translated sheet; the first sheet is binary by the two explicit cases in `IsConstrainedBurningRank`. `HasUnitSlip` checks both actual incoming edges, `NoEvenSlipSaddle` rules out the two orders of opposite nonzero increments when the gap is even, and the constraint on the rank demands the slip sign only on the decreasing choices used for the burn. It demands both choices at black sites. The output is a rank for the rounded field, so it also asserts the binary Laplacian. For `isBurningRank_alternateCompanion_midpoint` the second sheet is not a separate witness; its binary hypothesis follows using the prescribed offset.

The even non-affine calculation takes the two source generator identities with possibly distinct constants for the standard at the two generators. It does not assume any shift constant, rank or recurrence for the alternate. The Farey determinant and interior inequalities are the hypotheses used for the transverse difference and integer-gradient exclusion respectively.
