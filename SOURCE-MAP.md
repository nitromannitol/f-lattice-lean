## First proof's inputs

For `IsBurningRank.allowed`, the implicit field and rank, rank carrier and order are typing inputs from Section 9. The rank hypothesis expands to the two cases in Section 9 at every physical site. The finite vertex set and its nonemptiness are the induced subgraph in the conclusion of that argument.

For `finiteComparison_of_allowed`, the two fields and the finite set are the comparison data. The allowed-set hypothesis is exactly the forward-use hypothesis in `prop:recurrence`. The integer bound quantifies the pointwise form, with one hypothesis requiring that bound on the incoming boundary. The competitor assumption is restricted to the set because the proof only uses its Laplacian there. `IsBurningRank.finiteComparison` supplies the allowed-set hypothesis from the rank, and the separate endpoint module supplies the rank from the explicit endpoint function.

For `recurrent_iff_allowed`, the field and its superharmonicity are the only logical inputs. `Recurrent` uses precisely the global competitor premise and finite induced strong connectivity from the definition. The finite maxima equal the suprema because the test set and its incoming boundary are nonempty (the latter follows by taking an input from a site of minimal coordinate sum). `exists_source_component` takes a smallest predecessor set by cardinality in an arbitrary nonempty test set; all incoming edges from the original set into that component are internal. `Recurrent.allowed` uses superharmonicity outside that component and the failure of the allowed test inside it to show the indicator-subtracted competitor is superharmonic on the plane.
